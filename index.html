DEWGRID

Dewnode awakens

Deep in the dew-wet circuits, a leafy node named Dewnode stirs. Sap-sparks pulse through living traces. Four signal-wardens patrol the grid. Harvest the glow. Survive the chase. When a Sunburst flares - turn the hunt around.

How to Play

Move with Arrow keys or WASD. On touch devices, use the pad.

Collect all sap-sparks. Grab a Sunburst to briefly reverse the chase.

Side tunnels wrap left ↔ right. Pause with P or Esc.

  • Seek - locks onto Dewnode’s last known cell.
  • Weave - zigzags lanes, prefers turns.
  • Nest - circles a home node, then bursts out.
  • Dart - rushes corridors, rarely turns mid-run.
